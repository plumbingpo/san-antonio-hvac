Keeping Your McAllen HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of summer.

Small problems don't stay minor when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a miserable night.
