The Benefits of Professional HVAC Maintenance

Homeowners can take care of a short list: changing filters on time, keeping the outdoor coil clear, leaving registers open, and checking the drain. The rest takes instruments, because the problems that leave you stranded look perfectly normal. A capacitor that has lost 20 percent of its rating looks just like a new one. Low charge shows up only in superheat and subcooling readings. Cracked heat exchangers, weak flame signals, marginal pressure switches, and amp draws closing in on the breaker's limit are all invisible until measured, and all of them cost less to fix before they fail.

Burners, flame proving, venting, and draft deserve annual instrumented verification because the failure mode is not just a cold house, it is combustion products where they do not belong. HVAC Maintenance Prevention Tips

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Flush the condensate line every spring so algae cannot build up and clog it
  • Book spring and fall visits in the shoulder seasons, before the rush and before the extremes
  • Jot down any unusual sounds or run times, since they help your technician diagnose early
  • Replace your filter on a one- to three-month cycle; nothing else you do pays off more
  • If you have gas or other fuel-burning equipment, test your CO detectors every month
  • Keep supply and return registers open in every room, even the ones you rarely use
  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of vegetation and rinse the coil gently each spring
