Food Service Comfort

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. San Antonio HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we plan visits around your operating hours.

A kitchen stuck in negative pressure is miserable to work in, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the stuffy dining room fast. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. San Antonio HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched fast, throughout San Antonio and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we set up maintenance and monitoring to prevent a repeat, and each response is fully documented for your records.
